The Serengeti National Park is a large national park in northern Tanzania that stretches over 14,763 km² (5,700 sq mi). It is located in eastern Mara Region and northeastern Simiyu Region and contains over 15,000 km² (5,800 sq mi) of savanna. The park was established in 1940.
