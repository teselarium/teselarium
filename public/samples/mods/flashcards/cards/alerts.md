## Alerts

A blockquote whose first line is `[!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`,
`[!WARNING]` or `[!CAUTION]` becomes an alert:

> [!TIP]
>
> ## Study often
>
> A few cards every day beat all of them once a week.

An alert holds any markdown, headings included, and they belong to the alert.
