## Links

A link puts its text in square brackets and its address in parentheses:
[CommonMark](https://commonmark.org).

GitHub-flavored markdown also turns a bare address into a link:
https://github.github.com/gfm/.

A reference link names its address once, below the text: [the spec][gfm].

[gfm]: https://github.github.com/gfm/
