---
title: "Math"
question: "How do you write a formula inside a sentence?"
answer: "Between single dollar signs, as in $E = mc^2$"
---

## Formulas such as $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

A formula inside a sentence goes between single dollar signs: $E = mc^2$.

A formula on its own lines goes between double dollar signs:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$
