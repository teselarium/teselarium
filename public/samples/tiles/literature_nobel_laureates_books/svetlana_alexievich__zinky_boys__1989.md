Zinky Boys (Russian: Цинковые мальчики, romanized: Tsinkovye malchiki), also translated as Boys in Zinc is a 1989 documentary book  by Belarusian writer Svetlana Alexievich about the Soviet–Afghan War. The title refers to zinc-plated coffins in which the fallen soldiers were transported home to the Soviet Union as "cargo 200". The book collected anonymous memoirs of soldiers and relatives.
