The Goalie's Anxiety at the Penalty Kick (German: Die Angst des Tormanns beim Elfmeter) is a 1970 short novel by the Austrian Nobel Prize winning writer Peter Handke. It was adapted into a 1972 film with the same title, directed by Wim Wenders.
