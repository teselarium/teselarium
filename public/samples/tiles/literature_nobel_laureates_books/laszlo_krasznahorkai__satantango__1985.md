Satantango (Hungarian: Sátántangó, tr. "Satan's Tango") is a 1985 novel by the 2025 Nobel Prize winning Hungarian writer László Krasznahorkai. It is Krasznahorkai's debut novel. It was adapted into a widely acclaimed seven-hour film, Sátántangó (1994), directed by Béla Tarr. The English translation by George Szirtes won the Best Translated Book Award (2013).
