# Open, try and share a mosaic

You will open a mosaic, see the same data in another way, save your version and share it.

You only need OpenTesela open in your browser.

## Small glossary

- **Tiles** are your notes. These are markdown files with frontmatter.
- **Tile Collections** are just lists of tiles in yaml format.
- **Mods** are ways to show them: a list, a bookshelf, an interactive map, flashcards. These are javascript plugins.
- **Mosaics** pair a mod with a tile collection. A simple yaml file and one link to share.

## 1. Open a mosaic

1. In the sidebar, choose **Mosaics**.
2. Find **Nobel laureates on the map**.
3. Press **Open**.

You see a world map of where each Nobel Prize in Literature winner was born.

## 2. Read the bar

Above the map there is a bar with three buttons:

**Country Map** + **Nobel Prize in Literature laureates** → **Nobel laureates on the map**

- The first is the mod: _how_ the data is shown.
- The second is the tile collection: _what_ is shown.
- The third is the mosaic that joins them.

Press any of them to open a list of other choices.

## 3. Try another way to see it

1. Press **Country Map**. A list of mods opens.
2. Choose **Flashcards**.

The same writers now appear as a table, and you can study them as cards.

Look at the bar. The third button now says **Unsaved combination**, with a dashed border. You are trying something that is not saved yet.

In the lists, the number beside each choice, like `17/17`, tells you how many tiles fit it. A low number means the mod needs data the tiles do not have.

You can also change the collection of tiles. Press the second button to pick another one, or **None**.

## 4. Save the one you like

1. Press the pencil button, named **Edit as a new mosaic**.
2. Change the title to something you will recognize.
3. Press **Save**.
4. A box named **Save mosaic** shows what will be saved. Press **Save** again.

Your mosaic is saved in your browser.

## 5. Find it again

1. Choose **Mosaics** in the sidebar.
2. Your mosaic is in the list. It is marked **Added by you** and **Browser**.

Press **Open** to see it.

## 6. Share it

How you share depends on what you made.

**An unsaved combination.** Press the **More actions** button (`…`) in the bar, then **Copy link**. Send the link. Whoever opens it sees the same view.

**A mosaic saved in your browser.** It lives only in your browser, so a link to it would not work for anyone else. To share it:

1. In the **Mosaics** list, press **Export** on your mosaic and save the file.
2. Put the file on any website that serves files, such as GitHub Pages.
3. Share a link like this one, with the address of your file:

```
https://DEMO_URL/#/run?mosaic=https://example.org/my_mosaic.yaml
```

<details>
<summary>Why not just share the link?</summary>

A mosaic points to a mod and to a collection of tiles. When the file is on a website, anyone can follow those pointers. When it is only in your browser, nobody else can reach it.

</details>

## What you learned

- A mosaic pairs a mod with a tile collection.
- The bar lets you try other combinations.
- You can save a combination as a mosaic.
- A mosaic is shared by putting its file online and sharing a link.
